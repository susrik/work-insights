# Prepare a 1:1 or a project meeting

Both pages have the same layout: open threads by kind (agreements, goals,
work), a watch list (overdue, blocked, not mentioned for three weeks),
what was finished in the last 30 days, and mentions still waiting for
review.

## A 1:1

1. Open **People** and click the person.
2. Walk the **Agreements** list first: these are the things one of you
   said they would do. Mark done ones done from the thread panel
   (**All threads** opens the Threads page filtered to that person).
3. Look at **Not mentioned for 3 weeks**: things that have gone quiet are
   either done, forgotten, or blocked. Decide which.
4. **Done in the last 30 days** is what to acknowledge.

## A project meeting

1. Open **Labels** and click the project label.
2. Same structure, with owners shown on every line.
3. Items under **To review** are mentions intake found for this project
   that you have not confirmed yet; clear them on the Review page first if
   you want the picture to be accurate.

## Finding the people and labels

Intake creates a person for every folder under `notes.people_dir`. Labels
you create yourself; give them note-folder and Jira-project hints so new
threads pick them up without help.
